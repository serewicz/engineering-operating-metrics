# Governance Use Cases

## Purpose

Governance metrics help leaders see where engineering work lacks ownership, evidence, decision records, or review discipline.

## Use Cases

### Monthly CTO Review

Use the dashboard to discuss flow, quality, cost, and risk trends with engineering leadership.

### Technology Due Diligence

Use synthetic or exported metrics to show how engineering work is reviewed, governed, and connected to business risk.

### Operating Partner Review

Use team-level flow, cost, and governance metrics to identify where portfolio company engineering practices need support.

### AI Cost Governance

Track AI-assisted development cost by team and business area. Compare spend with quality and flow outcomes.

### Board Reporting

Summarize risk score, rework rate, review quality, and recommended actions without exposing raw pull request details.

## Governance Flags

Example flags:

- missing linked issue
- no explicit owner
- missing architecture decision
- high-risk change
- no test evidence
- AI cost unclassified
