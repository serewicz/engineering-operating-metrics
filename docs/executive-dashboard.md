# Executive Dashboard

## Purpose

The executive dashboard provides a concise operating view for CTOs, VP Engineering, boards, and operating partners.

## Top Summary Metrics

- Median cycle time
- Review quality score
- Rework rate
- Estimated engineering cost
- AI usage cost
- Risk score
- Recommended actions

## Views

### Flow

Shows whether work is moving predictably across teams.

### Quality

Shows review friction, rework, and post-merge bug signals.

### Cost

Shows engineering cost and AI usage cost by team.

### Risk

Shows high-risk pull requests and business areas with elevated technical risk.

### Governance

Shows missing ownership, missing issue links, missing architecture decisions, or other operating exceptions.

## Expected Outcome

The dashboard should support a monthly CTO operating review and a diligence conversation without requiring raw engineering data exports.
